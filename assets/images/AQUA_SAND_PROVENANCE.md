# Aqua v159 sand texture provenance

Aqua v159 performs a temporary A/B comparison of two Poly Haven sand textures.
Both sources are published under the Poly Haven **CC0** license.

## A — Aerial Beach 01
- Asset page: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_beach_01
- Author: Rob Tuytel
- License: CC0
- Runtime diffuse source used by v159:
  https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/aerial_beach_01/aerial_beach_01_diff_1k.jpg

## B — Sand 03
- Asset page: https://polyhaven.com/a/sand_03
- Author: Charlotte Baglioni
- License: CC0
- Runtime diffuse source used by v159:
  https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/sand_03/sand_03_diff_1k.jpg

The source images are conditioned at runtime through Lunar Ride's existing photo-texture pipeline and reduced to a 1024 square tile with a derived normal map. After visual selection, the preferred texture can be vendored locally if desired.
