# Aqua Rift v160 rocky seabed texture provenance

Aqua v160 replaces the v159 sand A/B experiment with a single rocky/rubble seabed treatment on both sides of the Glass Ocean road.

## Rocks Ground 04
- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rocks_ground_04
- Author: Rob Tuytel
- License: CC0
- Category: Ground & Terrain / Rocky Ground
- Runtime diffuse used by Lunar Ride: https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/rocks_ground_04/rocks_ground_04_diff_1k.jpg

The image is loaded at runtime and passed through Lunar Ride's existing `conditionTile()` pipeline, which prepares a tileable albedo and derives a normal map. No attribution is legally required by CC0, but the source is documented here for reproducibility and asset auditing.
